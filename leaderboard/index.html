Evidence responsiveness across diagnostic language models

DiagRare-Bench evaluates general-purpose and medically specialized open-weight checkpoints under the same diagnostic protocol. The table reports the primary top-1 accuracy, the fitted evidence coefficient, the randomized evidence effect, and sequential recovery when each estimate is available.

Rank is based on the fitted evidence coefficient among checkpoints with an identifiable primary estimate. Missing values are shown as em dashes and are not imputed. These quantities describe model behavior under the released protocol; they are not clinical safety scores.

What γevidence measures

The primary benchmark fits a two-feature Plackett-Luce model to ranked differentials. γevidence is the fitted change in pairwise diagnostic log odds associated with an evidence contrast after the disease-prior contrast is held fixed.

Independent validation

The paper tests the coefficient outside the rankings used to estimate it: on CUPCase and RareArena, under independently assigned evidence in a randomized experiment, and in a sequential diagnostic-revision task.
