Separating disease priors from patient evidence

DiagRare-Bench measures how strongly a model's diagnostic ranking changes with patient evidence after a disease-prior feature is accounted for, then tests that measurement under independent changes to the evidence.

Method

Measure the ranking, then change the evidence.

A two-feature Plackett-Luce model separates a disease-prior feature from case-specific evidence in the model's ranked differential. The key test is whether the resulting evidence coefficient predicts behavior in experiments that were not used to fit it.

01 · Estimate

Fit evidence responsiveness on 984 structured diagnostic cases.

02 · Transfer

Repeat the rank analysis on CUPCase and RareArena using a different evidence proxy.

03 · Randomize

Vary evidence strength and stated prevalence independently in a 3 × 3 experiment.

04 · Revise

Present evidence sequentially and measure recovery after an initial competing diagnosis.
